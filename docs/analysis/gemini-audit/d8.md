## What holds up
- bZx and Euler flash-loan accounting in 10-FO matches historical post-mortems and balance sheets exactly across every transaction step.
- The micro-lab (`ether_evm/index.html`) implements a completely self-contained, dependency-free Keccak-256 BigInt engine and interactive decoder that runs flawlessly offline.
- Address poisoning characterization rigorously distinguishes malicious look-alike lures from accidental clipboard hijackers based on independent on-chain evidence.
- LayerZero V2 message verification mechanics in Kelp DAO (nonce counter gap between Unichain and Ethereum) provide an airtight, objective proof of delivery forgery.
- NFT wash trading criteria clearly separate illegal self-financed volume manipulation from benign look-alikes like own-wallet reorganizations.

## Patterns to check elsewhere
- Check whether other labs rely on external web APIs (like `4byte.directory` or live block explorers) rather than self-contained offline containers.
- Check whether other graded lab points spoon-feed answers in the worksheet narrative rather than requiring students to analyze underlying data.
- Check whether other days use invalid compound confidence language ("Moderate to High") in violation of ICD 203.
- Check whether lecture slots cram multiple major concepts and interactive micro-labs into a single 50-minute block.
- Check whether snapshot data omissions in other labs are incorrectly described to students as inherent limits of blockchain transparency.
