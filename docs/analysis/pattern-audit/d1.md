## Candidate patterns

- Lessons that open with an Ethereum- or Tron-flavoured term (nonce, contract, sequencer, Super Representative) before the day that teaches that chain.
- One word, several meanings, unflagged ("nonce" three ways on day 1; "input" for hashes and transactions, `04-CP:22` against `02-IN:158`).
- Forward promises broken: a lesson defers an idea to a named later lesson that never covers it (`02-IN:505` → 01-CM, proof of work).
- Lessons with no figure where the idea is a flow (04-CP).
- Unanswered scenario openers: the scenario is asked on the first slide and the notes give no expected answer (`04-CP:23`, `01-CM:22`).
- "Memo" wording left over from before the 30 Oct direction.
- Day labs that ask for concepts taught days later, with no hint to bridge them.
- No lab debrief in the instructor answers (as in week 2).
