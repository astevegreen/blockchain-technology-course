1. **Circular deferrals.** Two lessons each say the other teaches an idea (proof of work: `02-BF:389`, `02-IN:505`, 01-CM),
   so nobody does. Day 1 found one end; day 2 closes the loop.
2. **Hidden slides hold the deferred idea.** The mining slides exist (`02-BF:1433`) but are hidden, while visible notes assume them.
3. **Recap Walkthrough "None".** Both recaps (`02-BF:674`, `06-BF:518`) leave the instructor nothing to say.
4. **Vocabulary bursts on advanced slides.** `06-BF:289`, `06-BF:379`, `05-BF:301`: four to six new terms on one slide, undefined.
5. **Asks without answers.** `05-BF:505`, `06-BF:201` (notes).
6. **The lesson answers the lab.** `05-BF:417`, `06-BF:477` reuse the lab's cases (criterion 10).
7. **Lab terms no lesson teaches**, glossed only in hints (fingerprint, BIP 48).
8. **No lab debrief**, and the worked example placed after the questions.
9. **Confidence rule stated two ways** (`06-BF:201` notes against `06-BF:477`).
