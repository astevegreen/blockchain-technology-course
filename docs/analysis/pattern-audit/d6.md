## Candidate patterns

1. Terms defined only in the speaker notes ("Define X briefly"), never on the slide (`02-FO:102`, `03-FO` portal, `01-FO` OSINT).
2. Hidden-slide fences that close early and leak retired slides after the recap (`01-FO:564/654/759/1150`). Check every lesson.
3. Asks live only in the notes. Good questions, but none is visible to the students.
4. Notes give no timings and no likely student questions (criterion 14 in all three lessons).
5. Recaps restate but carry no retrieval question (`02-FO:545`).
6. The lab's worked example comes after the questions and models only the easiest step.
7. Labs have no debrief tied to the lessons, and no on-track check for the core questions.
8. A case-study table introduces a heuristic the method lesson never taught (`03-FO:224`, `:226`).
9. Table-only lessons with no picture (01-FO).
10. Internal working notes in the speaker notes (slot labels, review tags: `02-FO:34`, `:389`).
