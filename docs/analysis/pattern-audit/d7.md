## Candidate patterns

- Questions live only in the notes: no slide asks the student anything, so a student reading alone never answers.
- The worked example is a finished product (a completed note) rather than the procedure from raw data to a row.
- Hard terms are defined only in the notes (Stratum, mixin, zk-SNARK, "nothing joins A and A'"), so the slide alone fails a first-time reader.
- Diagrams show the parts but not the hard rule (the key image that is refused, the cut in the mixer link).
- Confidence columns mix scale words with other phrases ("as strong as…").
- Study figures are given side by side with different bases and no sentence saying which matters.
- Notes have no timings and no likely student questions (criterion 14) in every lesson.
- Labs put the worked example after the questions and end with no debrief.
- Lab pages use a term no lesson taught ("toxic change").
