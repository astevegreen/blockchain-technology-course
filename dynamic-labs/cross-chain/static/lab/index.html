Day 8 lab: a cross-chain trace

This worksheet is being built. The query page and the saved results already work; the questions arrive once the lab's scenario is settled.

This lab reads a frozen snapshot of real transactions from two thefts that crossed chains: the Bybit theft of about $1.5 billion on 21 Feb 2025 [1], and the release of rsETH from Kelp DAO's bridge on 18 Apr 2026 (UTC, query 10) [2].

Every query is written for you. On the query page, press "Run" under a query, then read its output. You never edit the SQL. Write your answers down as short notes. Your instructor checks them with you.

The snapshot is frozen: every run gives the same answer. Query 16 says how far each chain was read. If the query page will not load, use the saved results: they show every query's output, so every question has the same answer there.

What you will do

  1. Read one contract the stolen funds passed through, and look up its function's 4-byte selector.
  2. Follow the funds across at least one swap and one bridge.
  3. Draw the cross-chain flow: each contract, the assets in and out, and the address on the far side.
  4. Write your assessment, with a confidence word for each judgement.
