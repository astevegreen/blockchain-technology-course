Day 10 final trace: the investment scam

One case, no worked example and no step-by-step questions. Follow the victims' money from their payments to where it is now, across two chains, using what weeks 1 and 2 taught. Keep your trace as short notes: one row per judgment. At the end you explain what you found to an instructor.

The case is invented. The Bitcoin leg runs on the course's own chain, not Bitcoin's; the Tron leg is an invented snapshot in the format of the day-5 and day-9 labs, not data from Tron. The names, addresses and amounts point to no real person or service. Its shape follows a real case: in October 2025 US prosecutors said Prince Group associates hid the source of scam profits by splitting cryptocurrency across scores of addresses and re-consolidating it into fewer ("spraying" and "funneling") [1], and the US and UK sanctioned the group the same day [2]. The related forfeiture case was not final as of 23 Oct 2026.

Your tools

  • Bitcoin explorer (mempool [3]). It is offline, so its dollar figures, fee badges and "Accelerate" buttons mean nothing. It shows times in your computer's time zone. Use a window at least 800 pixels wide.
  • Tron query page: ready-made queries over the Tron snapshot, run in your browser. You read them; you do not edit them. Times are UTC.
  • If either is down: the saved Bitcoin pages (times in UTC) and the saved Tron results show the same data, so every answer is the same there.
  • The Bitcoin leg's blocks were made seconds apart. Put Bitcoin events in order by block number; use times only to line the two chains up.

What to establish

  1. The path from the victims' payments to where the funds are now: one row per link, with what you see, the heuristic, what could make it wrong, and a confidence.
  2. Where the funds are at the end of the data, on each chain, and which outputs or amounts you could not follow.
  3. Which of those funds could still be frozen, and by whom.
  4. Who holds records that could identify the person behind the scam.
  5. Your bottom line, in a sentence or two, with a confidence word and what the data does not show.

Confidence words [4]

HighTwo or more independent lines of evidence agree, and no known failure of the reasoning applies.
ModerateOne strong line of evidence, or weaker lines that agree, but a known false positive could apply.
LowTiming, amount or behavior alone, or one unverified label such as an explorer tag.

Independent means a different kind of evidence, not a second look at the same transaction. Never give a percentage.
