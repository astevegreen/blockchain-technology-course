Investigate the classroom chain.

Search an address to plot its money flows. Circles are addresses (colored by co-spend cluster), squares are transactions. Double-click any address to expand one hop; click anything for details, then Expand flows (set hops) to follow a trail several steps at once. Right-click a node to trim it and everything downstream.

Switch to Timeline to lay transactions out by block height, oldest to newest. Or enter a from and to address and Find paths to see the flows between them.
