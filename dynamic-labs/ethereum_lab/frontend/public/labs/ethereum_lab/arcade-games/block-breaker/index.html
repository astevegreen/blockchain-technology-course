Ready?

Press Space or Launch Ball to start. Move the paddle with keys, mouse, or touch.

Controls

  • ← → or A D move the paddle.
  • Space launches the ball. During play, Space pauses or resumes.
  • Mouse or touch movement tracks the paddle horizontally.
  • Break every block to advance through 10 layouts. After level 10, the cycle restarts faster.
  • Catch falling powerups for multiball, paddle size changes, and ball speed shifts.
Play Again resets this local round only. It does not request or imply a new on-chain session.

Parent apps receive ready and completion messages. Direct browser play works without a parent frame.
