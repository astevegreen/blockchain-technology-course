Pool trading

The whole class trades against the lab's one pool, in three rounds. Each round you swap 1 ETH for cUSD in your own turn: one seat per block. Before you trade you work out what you should get and choose the least you will accept. After your block, you find your trade on the explorer and say whether someone sandwiched it.

Your seat's key comes from a test phrase printed in public software documentation. It guards nothing and exists only on this training chain.

1. Your seat

2. The round

Load your seat. The page then waits for your instructor to open a round.

3. Work out what 1 ETH buys

The pool pays out by the lesson's formula: for a WETH in, with x WETH and y cUSD in the pool, you get 997·a·y / (1000·x + 997·a) cUSD.

4. Choose the least you will accept

Your limit, below the quote the router gives when your trade is sent

5. Trade in your turn

The page waits for the block before your turn, asks the router for a quote, sets your limit from it, then signs and sends the swap so it lands in your block.

6. Were you sandwiched?

Open your block on the explorer and read the transactions in order. A sandwich is one account buying cUSD just before you and selling it back just after.

Your call

7. Your score

Per round: the prediction with its working, +1; the limit with its reason, +1; the right call with its transactions or its reason, +2; a sandwich called where there was none, −2. Time only breaks ties.
