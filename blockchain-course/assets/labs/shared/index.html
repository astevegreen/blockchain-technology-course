Confidence words

The course's scale (build spec, "Confidence scale"): High, Moderate, Low, never a percentage. One ink and a meter, not traffic-light colours: High means more evidence, not a better answer.

HighTwo or more independent lines of evidence agree, and no known failure of the heuristic applies..conf[data-level=high]
ModerateOne strong line, or weaker lines that agree, but a known false positive could apply..conf[data-level=moderate]
LowTiming, amount or behavior alone, or one unverified label such as an explorer tag..conf[data-level=low]

Colours

The Okabe–Ito set, chosen because it stays distinct under the common colour-vision deficiencies. Every object also has its own shape, so colour is never the only cue. Gold is the slides' bar colour and marks the lab's own controls only, never a blockchain object.
